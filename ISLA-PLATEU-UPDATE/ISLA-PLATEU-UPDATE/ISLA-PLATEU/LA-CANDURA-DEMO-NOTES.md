# LA Candura — ISLA PLATEU demonstration setup

- The existing `la-candura` restaurant row is reused. No duplicate restaurant is created.
- The database's existing business identity and phone are retained and completed from the official La Candura Resort and Hotel listing.
- Official Cloudbeds property metadata publishes the address as Candura, San Juan, Siquijor; phone `+63 915 748 8924`; and property coordinates `9.16677475, 123.51394653`. The map pin uses those published property coordinates.
- The official property listing describes an on-site restaurant, Asian cuisine and vegetarian options, with breakfast, brunch, lunch and dinner service. Exact daily restaurant hours and a current restaurant menu were not confirmed.
- The user supplied the LA Candura logo (black background with gold LA/CANDURA lettering). This exact image is used as the restaurant logo without alteration. The cover remains a generated illustrative image, not an official restaurant photograph.
- Eight demonstration menu items, prices and generated matching food photos are included. They are illustrative project content, not the restaurant's verified current menu or prices. The menu includes Asian-inspired mains, noodles/pasta, a vegetarian option, snack, dessert and non-alcoholic drink.
- Six sample reservation tables are created.
- A separate private payment placeholder is provided. It is deliberately non-scannable and marked `NOT A REAL QR / DO NOT SCAN`; the account name and number are placeholders. No real payment is possible from this demo configuration.
- Existing customer ordering, staff menu editing, reservations, order history and payment workflows are reused.
- Staff must be assigned to `la-candura` by an administrator before the Staff portal can manage this restaurant.
- The La Candura seed is additive and one-time for menu/table/payment records. It preserves later staff edits and does not modify PagudFood Cafe, The Cafe Infinite, Luca Loko or Dagsa Resto Bar.
